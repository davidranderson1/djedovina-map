Every change to prospects, contacts, tasks, owners and engagements is audit-logged in the database; this panel shows what happened since you last looked.

Research — the extract-reading queue

Unread parcels, workable sizes first (skip the mountain commons at the bottom). Open the land registry portal, read sheet B, type what you see — owners one per line as Name | share | flags (flags: no-oib, deceased, abroad), e.g. Ivan K. | 1/12 | deceased. Saving records the folio, links it to the parcel, and feeds the map's colors, filters and scoring.

People — everyone the registries name

Every enrolled holder gathered from the cadastre and your extracts, one row per real person, biggest holdings first. "Area held" is their share of every folio they sit on, summed. Search by name, address, or parcel number (e.g. 1924/4 — pick the municipality too, since numbers repeat across k.o.). Click a name to open their folios and parcels; ⚑ flags a parcel straight into the pipeline.

Activity — what changed in the registries

After the first pass, every parcel is re-read about every two weeks. When a possession sheet's holders change — a probate concluded, a share sold, a name corrected — it appears here as an event with the exact before and after. Watch a folio, parcel or person (👁) and its changes also ring the bell.

Features — what this app does, and how to use it

Djedovina's working tool, from the first parcel on the map to the signed contract. Each card says what a feature is for and how to use it; Show me jumps straight to it. New here?

Operations — the working pipeline

Every prospect, whatever its stage. Change a stage right here; it is journalled automatically. 📍 jumps to it on the map. Click a prospect's name to open its file — heirs, letters, documents.

Sales — deals and exits

Prospects from valuation (stage 9) through exit (stage 15): the money book. Set the market rate per m² and the offer per deal — every save is journalled.

Share ledger — who owns what

One row per registered holder, spelled exactly as the folio spells them. The bar shows how much of the folio is known, contacted, and agreed — the buying trigger is agreed = 100%. Rows are matched to real people automatically when the name is unambiguous; otherwise link a suggestion below the row.
